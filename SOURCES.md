# Sources for Daily Brief lessons (vetted list, 27 Nov 2026)

How sources rank. The writer uses the highest tier that covers the point. The critic checks every fact against tier 1 or 2.

## Tier 1: syllabus and official (facts, definitions, rules)
- CFA Institute: Level I to III topic outlines and curriculum; CFA Institute Research Foundation (free books and briefs); Standards of Practice Handbook.
- GARP: FRM Study Guide and Learning Objectives (Part I and II); GARP Code of Conduct.
- Central banks and supervisors: Federal Reserve, ECB, Bank of England, Bundesbank, BIS, IMF, FSB, BCBS, IOSCO, ESMA, SEC, CFTC, FCA, BaFin, FINMA.
- Data: News Lab `prices.csv` (market numbers), FRED, ECB Data Portal, Kenneth French Data Library, Damodaran Online (NYU) data sets.

## Tier 2: standard textbooks and university courses (explanations, worked examples)
- Hull: Options, Futures and Other Derivatives; Risk Management and Financial Institutions.
- Fabozzi: Bond Markets, Analysis and Strategies. Tuckman and Serrat: Fixed Income Securities.
- Bodie, Kane, Marcus: Investments. Damodaran: Investment Valuation (and his free NYU lectures).
- Ang: Asset Management. Grinold and Kahn: Active Portfolio Management.
- Free courses: Yale ECON 252 Financial Markets (Shiller), MIT OCW 15.401 Finance Theory.

## Tier 3: podcasts and practitioner media (topic ideas, real-world examples, how pros talk)
Used for ideas, examples and desk vocabulary only. Never copied, never the only source for a fact; any fact taken from them is checked against tier 1 or 2 and credited by name in notes.md. Check the show is still running "as of" the date used.
- Bloomberg Odd Lots (markets plumbing, desk language)
- NPR Planet Money and The Indicator (plain-English economics)
- Masters in Business (Barry Ritholtz; interviews with investors)
- Capital Allocators (Ted Seides; institutional investing, allocators)
- Invest Like the Best (Patrick O'Shaughnessy; investors and operators)
- Flirting with Models (Corey Hoffstein; quant and systematic strategies)
- Top Traders Unplugged (trend following, macro, volatility)
- Rational Reminder (evidence-based investing, academic research)
- FT Unhedged (markets commentary)

## Tier 4: Ehsan's own material (required supporting source: always consulted, never the only one)
- Repo `Ehsanaminia/fund-manager` (his "Fund Manager" / Asset Manager plan, 18 Oct 2026): `docs/curriculum.md` (120 lessons, CFA Level I topics, interview question per day), `docs/plan.md` (goals, target roles, reality check), `docs/game-spec.md`, `docs/data-plan.md`. Treated like tier 2: facts still cross-checked against tier 1.

## Not used as evidence
Blogs, forums, social media, unsourced articles, AI-generated summaries, prep-provider marketing pages (prep providers only to cross-check syllabus lists).
