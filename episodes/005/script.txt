Part three. Your first big question. Here it is. Explain to a friend, in about a minute: what does an asset manager do, and what are the main things they invest in? Use the words from this week. Say it out loud now.

[pause 45]

Here is a model answer, and yours may be different. An asset manager looks after other people's money. They decide how to split it between different assets, which is called asset allocation. The main ones are stocks, which are small pieces of ownership in companies, and bonds, which are loans to governments or companies. They often package the money in funds, such as E T Fs, which let many investors share a basket. They try to earn a return while controlling risk, and they are paid a yearly fee. Notice how it follows our pattern: what it is, how it earns, and what the risk is.
