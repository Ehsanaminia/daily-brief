Part two. Your first big idea. What is an asset manager?

Start with the word asset. An asset is anything that you own that has value, or that can make you money. A house is an asset. A share of a company is an asset. A bond is an asset. Cash in the bank is an asset too.

Now imagine your neighbor has a hundred thousand dollars. They don't know what to do with it. They don't have time to study companies, or watch markets every day. So they give the money to a professional, and say: please look after this and make it grow. That professional is an asset manager.

An asset manager does three things. One. They decide where to put the money: some in stocks, some in bonds, some in other things. That decision is called asset allocation, and it is the single most important decision in investing. Two. They choose the specific investments. Which companies? Which bonds? Three. They watch the risk. Risk means the chance that things go worse than you hoped. If markets fall like yesterday, a good manager is calm, because they planned for it.
