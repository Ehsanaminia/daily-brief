# Interview question bank (filled as the course goes; each lesson day adds one)

## Ep 6 (Interest)
**Q: What is an interest rate, and why does an asset manager care about it?**
Model answer: The price of borrowing money, as a percentage per year; the borrower pays, the lender receives. A manager cares because (1) it moves bond prices (rates up, existing bonds down), (2) the safe rate is the yardstick every other investment is compared against, (3) it sets how expensive borrowing is for companies, so it affects profits. Example: US 10Y fell from 5.154% (13 Nov) to 4.848% (25 Nov). Pattern: what it is, why it matters, an example.

## Ep 7 (Present value)
**Q: What is one dollar received in ten years worth today?** (also in fund-manager curriculum, day 5)
Model answer: It depends on the discount rate, the return I could earn elsewhere at the same risk. Discount it: 1/(1+r)^10. At 5% about 61 cents. Riskier payment, higher rate, lower value; the further away, the more the rate matters. That is also why bond prices fall when rates rise. Pattern: "it depends", on what, then a number.

## Ep 8 (Annuities and perpetuities)
**Q: How do you value a stream of cash flows that never ends, and what can go wrong?**
Model answer: A constant perpetuity is worth payment / discount rate (50 a year at 5% = 1,000). If it grows steadily, next payment / (rate - growth): the Gordon growth model. Risk: as growth nears the discount rate the value explodes (4/(8%-3%) = 80, but 4/(8%-7%) = 400), so keep long-run growth below the economy's growth and show a sensitivity table. Pattern: formula, example, risk.
