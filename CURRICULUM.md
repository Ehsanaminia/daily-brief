# Curriculum v2 (27 Nov 2026): from beginner to professional asset manager

Rebuilt around Ehsan's request: every asset class from the basics to professional level, how classes interact, how to buy and sell, and interview questions in every lesson. Week 1 (episodes 1 to 5) is done and approved. Mon to Thu: lesson days (news, lesson, interview corner, quiz). Friday: week review plus the weekly Myth vs Math report and growth ideas. About 45 weeks, 225 episodes.

## The template for each asset class (about 10 lesson episodes)
1. **What it is and who uses it** (beginner)
2. **What drives returns and risks**
3. **Key numbers and jargon** (how to read it)
4. **Valuation and analysis** (professional tools)
5. **Professional practice**: benchmarks, strategies, how managers differ, what good looks like
6. **How to buy and sell**: where it trades, instruments (direct, funds, futures), costs, liquidity, settlement, taxes
7. **When and what to consider**: timing versus rules, phasing in, the pre-trade checklist (PRETRADE_CHECKLIST.md), common mistakes
8. **Case study**: a real episode in history (what happened, what pros learned)
9. **Role in a portfolio and links with other classes** (correlation, in which conditions it helps or hurts)
10. **Interview workshop**: 5 to 8 questions on this class, beginner to hard

## Standing features in every lesson episode
- **Interview corner:** one real question, a model answer, a pause for Ehsan's own answer. Questions go in INTERVIEW_BANK.md.
- **Pre-trade lens:** from week 4 on, each new instrument ends with "what would I check before trading this?"
- **Interactions line:** "how this moves with stocks, bonds and gold" once it is known.
- **Vocabulary:** new words go to GLOSSARY.md; Friday reviews reuse them.

## Friday
News week in review, vocabulary sprint and quiz of the week, the weekly Myth vs Math report (numbers from `mythvsmath/tracker` and `ideas/PERFORMANCE-*.md`), and 2 or 3 growth ideas grounded in those numbers.

## Rules for changing this plan
- If a topic is too easy or too hard, adjust the coming weeks, never the finished ones.
- A big market event can replace a lesson for a day when it teaches something important; move the lesson to the next day.
- Everything is education, not advice: the course teaches how to think and what to check, not what to buy.
