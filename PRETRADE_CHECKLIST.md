# Pre-trade checklist (taught again in every asset class)
Before any transaction a professional asks:
1. **Purpose:** why this asset, what role in the portfolio (return, income, protection, diversification)?
2. **Size:** how much, as a share of the portfolio, and what is the maximum loss I accept?
3. **Instrument:** direct holding, fund/ETF, futures or other? What exactly am I exposed to?
4. **Costs:** fees, bid-ask spread, commissions, taxes, currency conversion, fund expense ratio.
5. **Liquidity:** how fast and at what price can I get out in a bad market?
6. **Where and how:** exchange or over-the-counter, order type (market, limit), broker, settlement and custody.
7. **Timing:** am I forced to act now (cash need, rebalancing rule) or just guessing? Phase in or all at once?
8. **Risks:** what can go wrong (market, credit, liquidity, currency, inflation, counterparty)? What would change my mind?
9. **Rules and fit:** client mandate, limits, regulation, tax situation, conflicts of interest.
10. **Exit plan and review:** when will I review, and what is the plan to reduce or sell?
Educational, not financial advice.
