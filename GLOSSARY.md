# Glossary (every term taught so far; reuse in quizzes)
| Ep | Term | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Market | A place where people buy and sell something; price depends on who wants it more |
| 1 | Asset | Anything you own that has value or can make you money |
| 1 | Asset manager | A professional who looks after other people's money: allocates, picks investments, watches risk |
| 1 | Asset allocation | Deciding how much money goes into stocks, bonds and other things |
| 1 | Risk | The chance that things go worse than you hoped |
| 1 | Fee | A small yearly percentage of the money managed, paid to the manager |
| 1 | Index | A score that tracks a group of companies, like a team average |
| 1 | Bond | A loan you give to a government or company; they pay you interest |
| 2 | Share / stock | A small piece of ownership in a company |
| 2 | Shareholder | A person who owns shares |
| 2 | Dividend | A share of profit a company pays to its owners |
| 2 | IPO | The first time a company sells its shares to the public |
| 2 | Stock exchange | An electronic marketplace where shares are traded |
| 3 | Currency (exchange rate) | How much of another currency you need to buy it |
| 3 | Face value | The amount a bond pays back at the end |
| 3 | Coupon | The yearly interest a bond pays |
| 3 | Maturity | The date a bond's loan ends |
| 3 | Issuer / bondholder | The borrower / the lender |
| 3 | Credit risk | The risk the borrower does not pay you back |
| 3 | Rate and price see-saw | When rates go up, existing bond prices go down, and the reverse |
| 4 | Volatility | How much and how fast a price swings up and down |
| 4 | Fund | A basket of investments that many investors share, run by an asset manager |
| 4 | Mutual fund | A fund priced once a day, after markets close |
| 4 | ETF | A fund that trades on the stock exchange all day, like a share |
| 4 | Passive / active | Passive copies an index; active tries to beat it |
| 4 | Expense ratio | The yearly fee of a fund, as a percentage of your money |
| 4 | Diversification | Not putting all your eggs in one basket |
